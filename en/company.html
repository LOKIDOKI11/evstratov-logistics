Questions before we start.

What information is needed for a quote?

Origin and destination, cargo type, packaging, weight, volume or dimensions, preferred mode and deadline. Tell us whether you require customs clearance and door delivery.

Do you handle dangerous or oversized goods?

Yes, subject to prior agreement of cargo characteristics. ADR goods, chemicals, batteries and oversized items require suitable transport, permits and handling arrangements.

What are the timing guidelines?

Published China guidelines: air 3–7 days; China–Moscow road 12–18 days; rail 18–25 days; sea combined with road or rail 30–45 days. Season and customs workload affect actual timing.

How is cargo protected?

Basic carrier liability applies; full cargo-value insurance can be arranged separately. Conditions and limits are recorded in the contract / CMR. Counterparties and route risks are checked before dispatch.

Can payment be staged or made in foreign currency?

Payments may be in CNY, USD or RUB, with an advance and the balance after cargo arrival. Bank transfers under contract may be with or without VAT. Terms are agreed for the specific project.
