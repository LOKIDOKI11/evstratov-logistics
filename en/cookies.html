Cookies and browser storage

1. Necessary preference

A browser-local preference remembers your choice for up to 180 days. If local storage is unavailable the page explains this and does not silently enable optional statistics.

2. Optional demonstration counts

If allowed, only aggregated interaction counts are stored locally for up to 30 days. They are not shared with a server, do not identify visitors and do not contain enquiry fields. The demo dashboard illustrates interface behaviour rather than shared website traffic.

3. Change your choice

Use Cookie settings in the footer. Rejecting or withdrawing optional consent clears the counts. No external analytics is connected. Hosting providers may process technical requests under their own policies.

4. Calculator

Estimate inputs are processed in memory only; no cookies or local storage are used to save them.
