Privacy policy

1. Operator

The operator identified on the official source website is individual entrepreneur Sergey Sergeyevich Evstratov. Contact: evstratovcargo@gmail.com. Address: Moscow, Tsvetnoy Boulevard 25, building 1, office 80. INN / OGRNIP registry identifiers have not been confirmed for this demonstration.

2. Enquiry drafts

The form uses your name, phone or Telegram contact and shipment description only to compose a draft in your browser. The fields are not submitted to the website, stored in localStorage or included in statistics. The draft remains in the current tab until you close or reload it. Do not enter passport, payment, health or other sensitive information.

3. Sending a message

Email opens your mail application with a draft. Copying text uses the clipboard. You choose whether to send it and which service to use. Email and messenger providers process data under their own policies; review them before sending.

4. Technical data and hosting

GitHub Pages serves this demonstrator and receives technical requests, including IP addresses and browser headers. Hosting log retention and processing arrangements have not been verified by the operator. This site does not claim that production infrastructure or Russian data-localisation requirements have been confirmed.

5. Optional browser statistics

No external analytics counter is connected. Optional demonstration event counts stay on this device. They do not contain form values, IP addresses or referrer history. They are enabled only after consent and retained for up to 30 days; the preference is kept for up to 180 days. Rejecting or revoking clears local counts.

6. Estimate demonstrator

The calculator processes route, cargo weight, volume, priority and the special-cargo flag locally. Values are not transmitted or stored. It uses fictional coefficients, not AI inference or live rates.

7. Your choices and rights

Use Cookie settings to reject optional statistics or withdraw permission. Contact the operator to request information, correction, withdrawal, restriction or deletion relating to an enquiry you actually send. Processing retention periods and production arrangements must be confirmed before a live lead-collection service is launched.

8. Status of these documents

English reference version for the demonstration. Actual processing purposes, retention, processors, localisation and regulatory notices require confirmation by the operator before commercial launch.
